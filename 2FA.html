Set Up Two-Factor Authentication on Microsoft 365

Protect your account with the Microsoft Authenticator app — takes less than 5 minutes.

ℹ️
What is 2FA / MFA? Multi-factor authentication adds a second layer of security to your Microsoft 365 account. Even if someone has your password, they cannot sign in without also approving a notification on your phone.
1
Install the Microsoft Authenticator app
1
Download and install the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone:
 iPhone — iOS App Store ▶ Android — Google Play
2
Open the app and sign in with your Microsoft 365 work email and password when prompted, or tap Add account if asked.
3
Allow the app permissions for notifications when prompted — this is required to receive approval requests.
2
Start the setup on your computer
4
On your computer, sign in to your Microsoft 365 account at office.com or open Outlook. You may see a prompt automatically — "Help us protect your account."

If not, go to: aka.ms/MFASetup
5
Click Next on the "Help us protect your account" screen.
6
Select Microsoft Authenticator as your method, then click Next.
3
Scan the QR code and verify
7
A QR code will appear on your computer screen. Open the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone and tap the + (Add account) button → Work or school account → Scan QR code.
8
Point your phone camera at the QR code on your computer screen. The app will add your account automatically.
9
Click Next on your computer. A test notification will be sent to your phone — tap Approve in the Authenticator app.
10
Click Done on your computer. 2FA is now active on your account.
✓
You're all set! From now on, when you sign in to Microsoft 365 you will receive an approval notification on your phone. Tap Approve to complete sign-in.
⚠️
Getting a new phone? Before switching phones, make sure to transfer or back up your Authenticator accounts. Contact One I.T. Pro and we will help you re-enroll.
ℹ️
Got a new phone? You will need to re-enroll Microsoft Authenticator and link it to your Microsoft 365 account again. Follow these steps.
1
Install the app on your new phone
1
Install Microsoft Authenticator on your new phone:
 iPhone — iOS App Store ▶ Android — Google Play
2
Allow notifications when the app asks for permissions.
2
Remove the old device and re-enroll
3
On your computer, go to aka.ms/mysecurityinfo and sign in with your Microsoft 365 account.
4
Find your old Authenticator app entry and click Delete to remove it.
5
Click + Add sign-in method → choose Authenticator app → follow the on-screen steps to scan the new QR code with your new phone.
6
Approve the test notification on your new phone and click Done.
✓
Re-enrollment complete. Your new phone is now linked to your Microsoft 365 account.
⚠️
Can't sign in to remove the old device? If you are locked out because you no longer have access to your old phone, contact One I.T. Pro and we will reset your MFA from the admin portal.

Template — First-time 2FA / MFA setup
Hi [Name],

Your IT administrator has enabled Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA/2FA) on your
Microsoft 365 account. This adds an extra layer of security so that even if your
password is compromised, your account stays protected.

Please follow these steps to complete your setup:

STEP 1 -- Install the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone:
   - iPhone:  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/microsoft-authenticator/id983156458
   - Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.azure.authenticator

STEP 2 -- On your computer, go to: https://aka.ms/MFASetup
   Sign in with your Microsoft 365 work email and password.

STEP 3 -- Follow the on-screen steps:
   1. Click Next on the "Help us protect your account" screen.
   2. Select Microsoft Authenticator and click Next.
   3. Open the Authenticator app on your phone.
   4. Tap + (Add account) > Work or school account > Scan QR code.
   5. Scan the QR code shown on your computer screen.
   6. Approve the test notification on your phone.
   7. Click Done on your computer.

Once complete, every time you sign in to Microsoft 365 you will receive an
approval notification on your phone -- simply tap Approve to sign in.

If you run into any issues, please reply to this ticket and we will assist.

Thank you,
[Your Name]

Template — New phone / re-enrollment
Hi [Name],

Since you have a new phone, you will need to re-enroll the Microsoft Authenticator
app to continue receiving sign-in approvals for your Microsoft 365 account.

Please follow these steps:

1. Install Microsoft Authenticator on your new phone:
   - iPhone:  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/microsoft-authenticator/id983156458
   - Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.azure.authenticator

2. On your computer, go to: https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo
   Sign in with your Microsoft 365 work email and password.

3. Find your old Authenticator entry and click Delete to remove it.

4. Click + Add sign-in method > Authenticator app and follow the steps
   to scan a new QR code with your new phone.

5. Approve the test notification and click Done.

If you are locked out and cannot sign in, please reply to this ticket right away
and we will reset your MFA from the admin portal.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
