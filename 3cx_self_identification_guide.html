Record Your Name in 3CX

Add your self-identification message from a desk phone or the 3CX mobile app.

🎙️ About 2 minutes
What is a self-identification message? It is a short recording of your name. It allows callers to find you in the dial-by-name directory, if your company uses that feature.
Before You Begin
☎
Desk telephonePress the MESSAGE button to enter voicemail.
▣
3CX mobile appOpen the 3CX mobile app, open the dial pad, and dial 9999.
The Short Version
After entering your voicemail PIN
9Options→5Self-ID→0Record
Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Open your voicemail.

    Using the 3CX mobile app: open the app, open the dial pad, and dial 9999. Using a desk telephone: press the MESSAGE button.

  2. Enter your voicemail PIN.

    When prompted, enter your PIN and press #.

  3. Open the self-identification menu.

    Press 9 for Options, then 5 for the Self-Identification Message.

  4. Record your name.

    Press 0, wait for the tone, and clearly say your first and last name.

  5. Stop and save.

    Press # to finish recording, then press 0 to save it.

Done! Your recorded name is now available to the 3CX dial-by-name directory, when that feature is enabled by your company.
Important: This does not change the voicemail greeting callers hear when you do not answer. To update that message, use the separate 3CX voicemail greeting guide.
