Join Your Caregiver Team on Microsoft Teams

Send reports, forms, and messages securely — from any device, in just a few minutes.

💬  Microsoft Teams
Welcome! Care 4 Soul has invited you to join a private Microsoft Teams channel where you can share caregiving reports, forms, and messages with the office. You do not need a Microsoft account or any paid subscription — you can join with the email address where you received your invitation. This guide walks you through everything, step by step.

 iPhone & iPad — App Store ▶ Android — Google Play
💻 On a computer? You don’t need to install anything — you can use Teams right in your web browser. Just follow Step 1 below and choose “Use the web app instead.”
How to Sign In — Step by Step
1
Open the Invitation Email
a
Check the inbox of the email address Care 4 Soul used to invite you. Look for a message titled “You have been added as a guest to SFC-C4S in Microsoft Teams.”
b
Open it and click the purple “Open Microsoft Teams” button.
c
Don’t see the email? Check your Spam or Junk folder. If it’s still not there, contact Care 4 Soul and ask them to re-send the invitation.
2
Choose How to Open Teams
a
On a phone or tablet: if you installed the Teams app, it will open automatically. If not, you’ll be prompted to download it — use the store links above.
b
On a computer: you’ll see a choice — you can download the app, or click Use the web app instead to open Teams right in your browser. Either works.
3
Get Your Sign-In Code
a
Enter your email address if asked, then choose “Send code.” Microsoft will email you a single-use code to sign in — you don’t need to remember a password.
b
Go back to your email inbox. Look for a message from Microsoft titled “Your single-use code.” It contains a 6-digit number (for example, 842 854).
c
Type that 6-digit code back into the Teams sign-in screen and continue.
⚠️ Keep your code private. Microsoft will never ask you for this code by phone or text. Only type it into the official sign-in screen.
4
Review & Accept
a
Microsoft may ask you to confirm your security info is accurate — if it looks correct, choose “Looks good!”
b
You’ll see a “Permissions requested by Care 4 Soul” screen. This is normal — it lets you join their Teams workspace. Review it and click “Accept.”
✅ That’s it! You’re now signed in. Teams will open to the SFC-C4S team and the General channel.
What to Expect Once You’re In
Inside the SFC-C4S Team
💬
The General channel is where you’ll post. Type your message in the box at the bottom that says “Message in General” and press send.
📎
To send a report or form, click the paperclip (attach) icon near the message box, choose your file or photo, and send it. You can attach documents, PDFs, or pictures.
📷
On your phone, you can snap a photo of a paper form and send it directly in the channel — tap the paperclip, then the camera.
👤
You’ll appear as a guest in the team. You can see and post in the General channel, but you won’t have access to Care 4 Soul’s internal files or other channels — this keeps everyone’s information secure.
🔒
Signing in again later: each time you sign in, Microsoft will email you a fresh single-use code. Just repeat Step 3 — there’s no password to remember.
📱 Tip: Turn on notifications in the Teams app so you don’t miss messages from the Care 4 Soul office. On your phone, allow notifications when Teams asks during setup.
